Troubleshooting

Walk this folder and everything below it, picking up files added or removed outside the uploader and building folders now instead of when each is first viewed. Useful after copying in many folders at once. Folders already up to date are skipped.

Not needed in normal use. If thumbnails look wrong or stale after a rescan, a rebuild deletes this folder's thumbnails and atlases and builds them all again from the originals. This folder only; subfolders are not touched. Large folders take a long time.

NUCLEAR OPTION

Deletes the thumbnails and atlases of this folder and of every folder below it, then builds all of them again from the originals. Originals, trash and hidden settings are not touched. Folders are done one at a time; each shows "Building" from the moment its turn comes until it finishes. On a large tree this takes hours, and there is almost never a reason to do it.
